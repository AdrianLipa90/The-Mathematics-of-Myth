\section{Mayor Formalism v0.1}

Adrienne Mayor's research programme repeatedly compares natural, historical,
material, or technological observations preserved in cultural narratives with
evidence obtained through independent modern disciplines.  We formalize that
method without assuming literal historicity or an invertible cultural channel.

Let
\[
M=\mathcal{T}(E,C)
\]
denote a cultural representation $M$ generated from a source phenomenon $E$
under transmission conditions $C$.  Version 0.1 makes no assumption that
$\mathcal{T}$ is lossless, lossy, or invertible.

Given independent evidence $X$ and competing hypotheses
$\mathcal H=\{H_1,\ldots,H_n\}$, the reconstruction problem is
\[
(M,X)\longrightarrow\mathcal H,
\]
with hypotheses constrained by both evidence channels.

\subsection{Typed relation signatures}

Each source is mapped to normalized relations
\[
r=(s,k,t,o),
\]
where $s$ and $t$ are semantic roles, $k$ is a relation type, and $o$ is an
optional order index.  For narrative graph $G_M$ and independent graph $G_X$,
the retained invariants are
\[
I(M,X)=E(G_M)\cap E(G_X).
\]

A simple overlap diagnostic is
\[
J(G_M,G_X)
=
\frac{|E(G_M)\cap E(G_X)|}
{|E(G_M)\cup E(G_X)|}.
\]
This is a structural score, not a probability of historical truth.

\subsection{Specificity against controls}

For preregistered negative controls $G_{C_i}$,
\[
\Delta_{\rm spec}
=
J(G_T,G_X)
-
\max_i J(G_{C_i},G_X).
\]
A positive margin indicates only specificity relative to the selected
controls.

\subsection{Research-domain projections}

Geomythology is represented through structured event signatures containing
location, chronology, process class, transformation sequence, observable
effects, and material constraints.

The Styx/Mavroneri programme is represented as a multi-channel constraint
system
\[
D_{\rm Styx}
=
D_T\oplus D_G\oplus D_{\rm tox}\oplus D_H,
\]
for textual, geological, toxicological, and historical evidence.

Artificial beings are represented as agent architectures
\[
\mathcal A=(B,S,A,G,C,V),
\]
for body/substrate, sensing, action, goal, creator/control relation, and
vulnerability.

Amazon/Scythian evidence is represented as a multiplex spatiotemporal graph
\[
G_A=(V,E,\lambda,\tau,\sigma),
\]
with independent textual, iconographic, archaeological, weapon, and
osteological channels.

Representation of extraordinary bodies can be treated as a mapping from
coded visual/social features to a reception vector, while ancient
self-medication reports can be represented by species--substance--behavior--
context--outcome tuples.

\subsection{Epistemic boundary}

The shared pipeline is
\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{representation}
\to
\mathrm{typed\ graph}
\to
\mathrm{independent\ graph}
\to
\mathrm{invariants}
\to
\mathrm{negative\ controls}
\to
\mathrm{constraints}.
}
\]

A relation match does not establish causal transmission, historical identity,
or a physical law.  No cosmological or fundamental-physics mechanism is
assumed in Mayor Formalism v0.1.
