# Evidence and provenance rules

The project uses six evidence tiers:

1. `PRIMARY_CANONICAL` — the source narrative itself or the earliest canonical textual layer used in the comparison.
2. `ANCIENT_EXEGESIS` — ancient interpretation of a primary source.
3. `LATE_TRADITION_TEXT` — a later textual recension preserving an older religious tradition.
4. `LATER_RITUAL` — later ritual, oral, or syncretic material.
5. `SCHOLARLY_SYNTHESIS` — modern scholarship explicitly reporting or synthesizing ancient source material.
6. `MODERN_COMPARATIVE` — project-level or modern comparative interpretation.

A lower/younger provenance tier may suggest a motif but may not silently rewrite an older source.

## Current source checks

- **Genesis 2:2-3**: supports completion/cessation at the six-to-seven boundary.
- **Hosea 6:2**: supports a third-day raising motif.
- **1 Corinthians 15:4**: supports a third-day resurrection motif.
- **Philo, Allegorical Interpretation I.2-5**: ancient exegesis explicitly rejects a naive six-clock-day creation reading, ties time to heavenly motion, and treats six/seven structurally.
- **Kojiki / Amaterasu cave**: supports withdrawal-darkness-emergence; does **not** supply the three-day duration used in the later comparison.
- **Blind-biwa Watamashi tradition**: later evidence for three days and three nights of mikagura before the cave still remains closed.

## Cross-cultural stress-test results

- **Inana's Descent (ETCSL c.1.4.1)**: primary Sumerian composition. Inana is a corpse on a hook; after three days and three nights the rescue procedure begins; Enki supplies life-giving plant/water and Inana later arises. This supports a three-interval threshold and a death-liminal-life order relation, but **not** the claim that revival occurs exactly at the third-day boundary.
- **Inana's seven gates**: the sixth gate removes the measuring rod/line; the seventh removes the final garment, after which judgment/death follows. Strong 6→7 boundary geometry, different boundary semantics from Sabbath.
- **Gilgamesh XI flood**: six days of storm/flood activity; on the seventh day the gale relents and the Deluge ends. Strong projected 6→7 active-to-cessation schedule. Historical independence from Genesis is not assumed.
- **Zoroastrian post-mortem interval**: three days/nights near the corpse; transition toward the Činwad bridge/judgment after the third night. Threshold analogy, not resurrection.
- **Katha Upanishad 1.1.7-9**: Nachiketas waits three nights in the house of Death; Yama then returns and grants three boons. Threshold analogy, not resurrection.
- **Kojiki opening**: three solitary deities appear/hide, followed by two further solitary deities. This is a genuine `3|2` prefix; no `1|0` continuation has yet been established.
- **Zoroastrian six/seven creation variants**: useful negative control. Both counts occur, but the seventh is not stably a cessation state.
- **Enūma Eliš VI-VII**: another negative control. Humanity is created in VI and VII is largely praise/names, but the name sequence begins already in VI, so the boundary is not clean.

## Current strongest formal claims

1. `3 -> threshold/regime change` occurs in several source-controlled traditions, but with different semantics.
2. `6 -> 7 cessation` has a particularly clean projected match between Genesis and Gilgamesh XI.
3. `6 -> 7 regime change` also appears in Inana's seven-gate descent, but the seventh means final stripping/death, not rest.
4. No verified full `3|2|1->0` cross-cultural isomorphism is claimed.

The statistical independence of traditions is a separate historical question and must not be inferred from formal similarity.
